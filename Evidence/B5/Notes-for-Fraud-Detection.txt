AI Being Weaponised
- Attacker's toolkit increased because of AI
- Attacks used to have wrong grammar, or too long emails
- But now AI can help with producing "professional" emails
- So Phishing is getting more sophisticated
- Voice Cloning: AI model does not have breath control, so voice can say a whole sentence without taking a breath, but still really hard to see it
- Deepfake: Fake live videos, if lose your id and someone tries to create an account with your id, they don't look like you but they can deepfake your face to try and impersonate you to bypass the live video  checks (biometrics), easier to do on webcam than phones,
- Automated Scam Scaling: use bots to mass send scams to a lot of people, and then call back those who tried to return the call, kinda like clickbait, attakcers will see if it's worth attacking or not
